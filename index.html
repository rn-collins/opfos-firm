Institutions of One · technical implementation by Aloha AI

One-Person-Firm OS

Portable operating infrastructure for solo consultants, fractional executives, creators, and portfolio professionals whose work has outgrown scattered tools and improvised routines.

Public service-architecture prototype · discovery required before any engagement
The design question

What should one person own, see, and control to run a serious practice?

The concept connects positioning, opportunities, delivery, evidence, administration, and carefully governed AI assistance into one reviewable operating architecture. It is not downloadable software and it does not promise autonomous operations.

Practice map

Clarify the work, audiences, offers, obligations, decision rights, and boundaries before configuring tools.

Command center

Design proposed schemas and views for priorities, relationships, projects, revenue, evidence, and recurring review.

AI workspaces

Define bounded research, operations, and communications assistance with sources, approvals, and human review.

Proposed engagement architecture

A scoped setup sprint, not a one-size-fits-all install

Discover the operating reality

Map current work, handoffs, tools, risks, information classes, and recurring points of friction.

Design the smallest useful system

Agree on supported workflows, ownership, permissions, acceptance criteria, exclusions, and portability.

Configure and test

Build only approved components, test them with sanitized examples, document decisions, and train the owner.

Review and hand off

Validate the system, document limitations, export what is portable, and establish a change and offboarding path.

Possible scopes

Different practices need different depths

Operating diagnosis

  • workflow and tool inventory
  • friction and risk map
  • prioritized implementation plan

Core workspace

  • approved schemas and views
  • weekly review structure
  • documentation and handoff

Governed AI assistance

  • bounded use cases
  • source and approval rules
  • testing and human-review controls

These are proposed scope patterns, not fixed packages or promises. Timing, price, deliverables, prerequisites, and support would be established in a written project-specific scope.

Important operating boundary

Do not place confidential, privileged, regulated, client, health, legal, financial, or other sensitive information into any proposed AI or SaaS workspace until the owner has approved data classification, vendor terms, retention and training settings, permissions, processing agreements, review rules, and incident procedures.

AI output does not replace qualified legal, clinical, tax, financial, security, accessibility, or other professional review. No system can truthfully promise that nothing will fall through the cracks.
